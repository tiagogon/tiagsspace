Mix

keys A · S · D

Untouched (4K Lento, 46 minutes). The envelope is the file's real loudness, drawn like the mix cursor: one closed polygon of straight segments. Mirrored = the same loudness above and below the spine; Stereo L/R = the left channel above, the right below (each in the chosen measure). Played = filled with ink. A click plays or pauses (the play / pause cursor); drag the playhead's edge to move through it; ←/→ ±10 s, Shift ±60 s once focused. The play button shows on touch only; AirPlay only where Safari finds a device.
